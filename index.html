What We Offer

Permanent Architectural
Lighting Services

One craft, done perfectly. Every service below is designed to make your home shine — safely, permanently, and on your schedule.

💡
Permanent Architectural Lighting
Our flagship service. Recessed LED fixtures installed along your roofline, fascia, and eaves — completely hidden by day, stunning by night. Engineered for Minnesota freeze-thaw cycles and rated for 50,000+ hours.
🔒
Security Lighting
Bright, motion-aware illumination placed at entry points, garages, and dark corners. Deters intruders and gives you peace of mind year-round — without the look of construction-site floodlights.
🎄
Holiday Lighting
Never climb a ladder again. Our permanent system handles every holiday — Christmas, Halloween, Fourth of July — with programmable color scenes and custom schedules. One install, every celebration.
🏈
Game Day Lighting
Custom color scenes built around your team's schedule. Switch to Vikings purple, Wild green, or Twins red with one tap on game day. Makes every kickoff feel like a block party.
🎨
Accent Lighting
Highlight your home's best architectural features — columns, dormers, stonework, landscaping. Elegant, custom illumination that adds depth and drama after dark.
📱
Smart Control Systems
Every installation pairs with an intuitive app. Set schedules, create scenes, adjust brightness, and control your lights from anywhere. Works with most smart home platforms.
