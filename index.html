How It Works

Four Steps To A Lit Home

1

Free Demo

We bring a lit demo board to your home and hold it against your actual roofline so you can see it before deciding.

2

Design

We measure your roofline, match your track color by RAL code, and map the layout.

3

Install

The track is mounted to fascia or soffit and the low-voltage line is run. Most homes finish in 1–3 days.

4

Control

We set up the Wi-Fi app with you, build your first scenes, and show you how to schedule them.
